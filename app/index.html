Transfer AMN

Requires the Trader role. The Proxy RPC rejects transfers from identities without it — try switching to the Auditor to see the rule enforced.

Send

What happens

  1. Proxy RPC authenticates you and checks the Trader role.
  2. The transaction executes off-chain in the private environment — seconds, negligible cost.
  3. It joins the next batch; sealing the batch produces a validity proof + state root.
  4. The proof is anchored to Ethereum. Your transfer details stay private.

Google access — who sees the console

Everyone signing in with another Google account gets the viewer experience (balance + stats) only. Because this app is fully static, the admin list ships in the code — to invite someone permanently, add their email to AUTH.admins in app/index.html (or ask for it to be added) and redeploy.

Chain controls

Chain state persists in this browser (localStorage). Reset wipes all accounts, transactions, and batches back to genesis.

RPC Console

Raw JSON-RPC against the Proxy RPC. In built-in mode, calls hit the in-browser chain; in External mode they go to your configured endpoint. Role rules apply either way the proxy defines them.

Network

The built-in chain runs the Prividium architecture entirely in your browser. When your hosted Prividium deployment is live, switch to External mode and point the app at its Proxy RPC.
