The Problem

Remittances are Indonesia's quiet lifeline — and it leaks at every step.

Millions of Indonesian migrant workers across Malaysia, Saudi Arabia, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Japan and Korea send home more than $16 billion a year. Incumbent corridors take 5–7% in fees, hold funds for days, and deliver into a system where nearly half of recipients have no meaningful access to banking. The value extracted by intermediaries leaves the country instead of reaching Indonesian households.

How It Works

From a worker's wallet abroad to family in Indonesia — in seconds.

Amanah pairs a mobile wallet with trusted physical distribution partners whose nationwide reach extends into the communities where remittances land and where banks are absent.

Send

A worker abroad converts wages to AMN through licensed, Sharia-compliant sending partners in key corridors.

Settle

AMN settles on Amanah's private chain in seconds, at negligible cost — no correspondent banks, no FX spread stacking.

Receive

Family members receive value instantly in the Amanah wallet, with rupiah access through a nationwide network of trusted local partners.

Grow

Balances held in AMN earn a halal return from sukuk-backed reserves — a savings layer for households the banking system never reached.

Private by Design

Transfers stay confidential end to end. Amounts and counterparties are shielded from intermediaries and onlookers on Amanah's private chain, while regulators retain the oversight the framework requires — privacy for families, not opacity for bad actors.

Yield-Bearing by Design

AMN holders share in the returns of the reserve — structured as Sharia-compliant profit-sharing on sukuk, not interest. No incumbent remittance provider or major USD stablecoin offers this to retail users.

Sharia-Compliant End to End

Reserves in sukuk and compliant instruments, governance under a Sharia Supervisory Board, and corridors built with Islamic banks on both sides.

Distribution at National Scale

Established nationwide networks provide the cash-in/cash-out layer — physical reach no standalone fintech can replicate.

Technology

A private chain, purpose-built for a national payment system.

AMN runs on a dedicated private chain provided by Abstract, rather than a public network. Amanah's Indonesian operating company validates and sequences transactions, manages collateralization and redemptions, and interfaces directly with banks and regulators.

Control & Oversight

Sovereign-grade control over validators and sequencing, with built-in oversight capabilities for regulators — designed to operate within Indonesia's regulatory sandbox framework.

Cost & Scale

Negligible, predictable transaction costs make sub-1% remittance pricing sustainable, with capacity to scale to national payment volumes and, in time, commodity trade settlement.
